Questions

Good to know.

Do I need Lightroom?

Yes. Use the free Lightroom Mobile app (Android / iPhone) with the DNG files, or Lightroom Classic / Lightroom CC on a computer with the XMP files. Both are included.

How do I receive the pack?

Tap “Buy now” and WhatsApp opens with your order. Once payment is confirmed, the files and an install guide are sent to you in the same chat.

Will my photos look exactly like the examples?

Every photo is different, so treat the preset as a starting point. You may need to adjust exposure and white balance a little for the best result.

Does it work on phone photos?

Yes. It works on phone photos and on RAW or JPEG files from any camera. RAW files give you the most room to adjust.

Can I get a refund?

Because the presets are digital files, sales are final. If something isn’t working, message on WhatsApp and Jawhar will help you set it up.
